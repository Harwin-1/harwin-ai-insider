The guide in one glance
🟢 GreenPublished datasheets and product pages, anything on our public website, general process questions, and drafts or notes that contain nothing from amber or red.
🟡 AmberInternal reports, pricing, stock, customer and supplier names, internal procedures, personal details. Approved tools only. Paste the minimum, strip identifiers, use made-up examples where they'll do.
🔴 RedCustomer drawings and specifications, export-controlled data, anything a customer contract restricts, passwords and credentials. Not in any AI tool unless IT has specifically approved it.

Classify by what a document contains, not who wrote it. If you're not sure, treat it as red and ask. Describe the information in your question; don't paste it.
