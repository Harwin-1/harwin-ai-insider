AI in plain English

The words that keep coming up, in one sentence each, with a Harwin example.
All tools · AI Library · Can I paste this? · All issues
Agent
An AI that doesn't just answer but takes steps on its own, like searching, reading files or sending a draft.
At Harwin: A scheduled ChatGPT agent that checks supplier news every morning.
Approved tool
An AI tool Harwin has checked and set up with a company account: ChatGPT Business, Microsoft Copilot (M365) and Claude Team.
At Harwin: Your Harwin Claude Team account, not a personal Claude Pro one.
Artifact
A finished thing Claude makes in a side panel, like a table, document or small tool, that you can edit in place.
At Harwin: Turning spec lines from a PDF into a comparison table.
Chatbot
AI you talk to one message at a time, with you reading every answer before anything happens.
At Harwin: Asking ChatGPT to shorten an email.
Connector
A link that lets an AI tool read your files in places like SharePoint or OneDrive. Goes through IT first.
At Harwin: Letting Claude read a SharePoint folder of procedures.
Context window
How much text an AI can hold in mind at once. Bigger means it can read longer documents in one go.
At Harwin: Claude reading a whole quality manual at once.
Copilot Chat
The free AI chat built into Microsoft 365. Since November it has hidden daily limits for people without a paid Copilot licence.
At Harwin: Summarising an Outlook thread.
Deep Research
A ChatGPT mode that spends a few minutes searching and gives a longer, sourced answer.
At Harwin: Scoping a new supplier before a meeting.
Effort
A Claude setting for how long it thinks before answering. More effort, better answers on hard problems, more cost.
At Harwin: Turning effort up for a tricky contract question.
Export-controlled
Information that law or a licence restricts, such as ITAR or EAR items. Always red: never into AI unless IT has approved it.
At Harwin: A drawing for a controlled defence part.
Hallucination
When AI confidently makes something up, like a figure, a policy or a source.
At Harwin: Inventing a delivery date. Always ask: where did this come from?
Human in the loop
A person checks and decides before anything important happens. Our standing rule: AI drafts, a person decides.
At Harwin: A person reviews every customer email an AI drafted.
Model
The engine behind the tool. Different models trade speed and cost for depth.
At Harwin: Haiku 5.5, Sonnet 5.5 and Opus 5.5 in Claude.
Model ladder
Start on the everyday model, drop down for quick jobs, step up only when the answer falls short.
At Harwin: Sonnet 5.5 by default, Haiku 5.5 for sorting, Opus 5.5 for hard problems.
Personal data
Anything that identifies a person: names, emails, phone numbers, addresses. Strip it out before you paste.
At Harwin: A candidate's CV details.
Prompt
What you type to tell the AI what you want. The clearer it is, the better the answer.
At Harwin: "Cut this to half the length. Keep every date and number."
Prompt library
A shared collection of prompts that work, so nobody has to start from scratch.
At Harwin: The Harwin AI Library.
Redact
Remove or replace sensitive details before sharing, for example [CUSTOMER A] instead of a name.
At Harwin: Swapping prices for [PRICE] before asking for a reply.
Role-play
Asking AI to act as a particular person so you can rehearse.
At Harwin: "You are our CFO. Ask me the hard questions."
Traffic-light guide
Harwin's green, amber, red rules for what can go into AI tools.
At Harwin: Datasheets are green, pricing is amber, customer drawings are red.
